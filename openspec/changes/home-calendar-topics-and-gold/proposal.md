# Home calendar, topic tabs and gold

Consume BE-P `home-topic-news-and-logistics-press`. News and logistics cards switch topics with
TabList (coloured topic tag per story in the all-topics tab). Add a tear-off wall calendar card
above gold: solar day, weekday, observance, lunar date (Hồ Ngọc Đức algorithm, UTC+7), Can Chi,
giờ hoàng đạo and a daily Vietnamese proverb. Gold becomes neutral with one tile per type,
large buy/sell figures and the buy–sell spread. The rail no longer sticks (it is taller than a viewport).
