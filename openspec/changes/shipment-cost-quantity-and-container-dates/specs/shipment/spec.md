# Shipment cost and container date UI

## Requirements

- Cost editor defaults quantity to 1 and shows unit price and computed total; edits and deletions preserve all saved quantities.
- Cost tables show quantity, unit price, and line total; group and shipment totals use the line total.
- The per-container date table uses fixed-width date controls; selecting or clearing a date does not resize its column.
- Date selection and clearing remain keyboard accessible, while the bulk date field remains available for typing.
