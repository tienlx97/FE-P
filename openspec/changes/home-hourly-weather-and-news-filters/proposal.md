# Home hourly weather and news filters

Consume BE-P `enrich-home-feed`: weather shows a 12-hour scrollable strip, feels-like, humidity,
wind, UV band and precipitation with condition icons; 3-day forecast gets icons. Headlines
(36 from four publishers) get publisher filters, a featured lead and colour-coded publisher
tokens with relative times; logistics gets Tất cả / Việt Nam / Quốc tế filters. Weather and
gold cards use blue and yellow category tints; likely rain (≥50%) is highlighted in accent.
