# Home requirements

- Weather shows the current condition icon, temperature, feels-like, humidity, wind, UV with WHO band and precipitation, a keyboard-focusable 12-hour strip ("Bây giờ" first) and a 3-day forecast; rain probability ≥50% is accent-coloured.
- Headlines show 8 stories initially with a larger lead, filter by publisher with per-option counts, and expand/collapse.
- Logistics filters Tất cả / Việt Nam / Quốc tế by article `region`, 8 stories initially.
- Each story shows a colour-coded publisher token and a relative publication time measured from when the client received the feed.
- Mobile has no horizontal page overflow; long filters scroll inside their card.
