# Calendar quote

The Home calendar card shows the solar weekday and day, the observance (if
any) and the lunar day/month as one pill underneath. The year Can Chi stays in
the header band; day and month Can Chi and auspicious hours are not shown.

Below that, "quote of the day": one quote with its attribution (well-known
people, Vietnamese proverbs/ca dao) as a hand-built semantic
figure/blockquote/figcaption. The quote is stable for the Vietnam day and
differs from the next day's; the list cycles without repeats.
