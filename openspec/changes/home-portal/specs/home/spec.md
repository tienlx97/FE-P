# Requirements

- Desktop: one news column (carousel plus three secondary news rows initially)
  beside notices and company calendar. Holidays appear only within the calendar.
- Additional news is available through an accessible expand/collapse button;
  featured IDs and secondary IDs do not overlap. No misleading news archive link.
- Mobile: stacked regions without horizontal overflow; readable full notice titles.
- Calendar starts in current Vietnam month; previous/next synchronize grid and
  agenda, including year boundaries. Empty months have an explanatory message.
- Verify scenarios in browser, capture desktop/mobile screenshots and pass full gate.
